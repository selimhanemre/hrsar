About HRS

A crew you can call, and a crew that shows up.

Family-owned since 2017, based in the Little Rock metro, and still one of the first things a customer says when we're done: "you actually came back."

Who we are

Started with two trucks and a promise: the parts come with the repair.

HRS was founded in 2017 by a family that had watched too many Arkansas restaurants shut down for a day — or a week — over a part that could have walked on the truck. The first rule was never written on a whiteboard. It was stock the truck for the repair, not an estimate of the repair.

Today the company runs a full commercial HVAC and refrigeration operation across the state: rooftop units, packaged systems, split-systems, ductless mini-splits, walk-ins and reach-ins, ice machines, display cases, and kitchen exhaust. The scale grew. The rule didn't change.

We're a small team of licensed, manufacturer-certified technicians — continuously trained on the latest HVAC and refrigeration technology — and we stay that way on purpose. The person on the phone is the person who owns the company, and the one on the roof is trained by the manufacturer of what's on your roof.

What we stand for

Three rules. No exceptions.

01

The repair comes with the part.

Every truck is stocked for the failures that happen most. If the fix needs a part we don't have here, we say so before we start — not after the second trip.

02

The estimate is the number.

A free, written estimate before work begins. If the diagnosis changes the scope, you hear it before the invoice — not after it.

03

The name is on every job.

Family-owned means every repair is attached to someone who owns this company, not a dispatcher who rotates tickets. We keep the records. You can ask for them.
