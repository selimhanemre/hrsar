About HRS

A commercial crew you can call, and a crew that shows up.

Family-owned since 2017, based in the Little Rock metro, and still known across Arkansas for one simple habit: answering the phone and finishing the fix on the first trip.

Who we are

Started with two trucks and a promise: the parts come with the repair.

HRS was founded in 2017 by a family that had watched too many Arkansas restaurants and commercial kitchens lose product for a day — or a week — over a part that should have ridden on the service van. Our first rule was stock every truck for the repair, not an estimate of the repair.

Today the company runs a dedicated commercial HVAC and refrigeration operation across Arkansas: rooftop units, packaged systems, split-systems, ductless mini-splits, walk-ins and reach-ins, ice machines, display cases, and kitchen exhaust. The fleet grew. The rule didn't change.

We're a tight team of licensed (#2428110), manufacturer-certified technicians — continuously trained on commercial HVAC and cold-chain hardware — so the person answering your call owns the outcome.

What we stand for

Three rules. No exceptions.

01

The repair comes with the part.

Every truck is stocked for the failures that happen most. If a fix needs a specialty OEM part, we tell you upfront — never after an unnecessary second trip.

02

The estimate is the number.

A free, written estimate before work begins. If the diagnosis changes the scope, your facility manager hears it before the invoice — never after.

03

Our name is on every job.

Family-owned means every repair is tied directly to ownership, not a rotating call-center ticket. We keep clean service records you can hand to any inspector.
