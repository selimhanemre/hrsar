Commercial FAQ

Straight answers before we roll a truck.

Do you offer Preventive Maintenance contracts for commercial facilities?

Yes. We tailor PM schedules around your operating hours and equipment load — covering RTUs, walk-ins, ice machines, and kitchen exhaust — with priority emergency dispatch, discounted emergency rates, and documented service logs for warranties and inspections.

How fast can you dispatch for an emergency commercial failure?

Our emergency line runs 24/7, 365 days including holidays. Central Arkansas commercial calls get priority dispatch, and because our vans carry high-failure OEM parts, most emergencies are fixed on the first visit.

Do you charge for commercial diagnostics or estimates?

Estimates are free — over the phone or on site. You receive a clear written quote before any repair or installation work starts, with zero surprise line items afterward.

Can you support health and fire code compliance for commercial kitchens?

Yes. We service vented kitchen hoods, baffle filters, make-up air balancing, and cold-chain temperature equipment, providing inspection-ready documentation and compliance photos on every visit.

What commercial territories in Arkansas do you cover?

Our home base covers Little Rock, North Little Rock, Sherwood, Benton, Maumelle, Jacksonville, and Bryant, and we dispatch statewide across Arkansas for commercial clients and multi-site accounts.
