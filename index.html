FAQ

The questions we get on every first call.

Do you cover my area?

Our core is the Little Rock metro — Little Rock, North Little Rock, Sherwood, Benton, Maumelle, Jacksonville, Bryant — and we serve the rest of Arkansas. Call and we'll be honest about timing for your address.

How fast can you get here in an emergency?

We run 24/7, 365 days, including holidays. Metro calls are dispatched priority, and because our trucks are stocked for common failures, most emergencies get fixed on the first visit.

What does it cost?

Estimates are always free — over the phone or on site. No charge for the diagnosis, and the written quote is final before we start. No surprises after the fact.

Do you work on residential systems?

We specialize in commercial systems, but split-systems and ductless mini-splits in smaller buildings are fair game. For a full residential install, we'll say so and point you where the fit is better.

What's in a maintenance plan?

Scheduled preventive visits, component checks, filter service, refrigerant tops, priority dispatch when something does go wrong, and a documented service history that supports warranty and insurance claims.
